## 1. The three content tiers

Definitions used: **General** = anyone can understand and want it ("how to do a backflip"). **Specialist** = only people who already train care ("why your corkscrew looks ugly"). **Informational/curiosity** = a surprising fact about the sport or body that needs no skill ("why short people tend to be better at tricking").

### 1.4 Recommended mix for taita.del

Assumption: you have reach (10M views) but no conversion. So the mix should *not* chase more reach; it should maximise follow-per-view.

| Tier | Share | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Specialist | 40% | Highest follow intent per view and your real moat (national rank 8th in 2025, 100+ students coached). Also feeds coaching revenue. |
| Informational | 35% | Lets non-trickers learn why you are worth following, and uses the Berkeley/CS/physics angle. Best "second hook" after a crash video. |
| General | 25% | Keep a reach engine, but only in **series form** (e.g. "learning X in N attempts"), never as one-off tutorials or standalone crash clips. |

This is my recommendation [inference], not an evidenced optimum. Treat the first 4 weeks as a test: log followers gained per 1,000 views for each tier and shift the split toward whichever wins. Do not tune on view counts alone.

## 3. Diagnosis: why a 10M-view crash converts at ~0.06%

What is evidenced vs inferred:

**Evidenced (practitioner reports, low rigor):** viewers often follow for one piece of content, not the account; a profile unrelated to the viral post lowers conversion; conversion comes more from consistent mid-performing posts than from one hit; typical view-to-follow rates are tiny ([Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/what-happens-when-you-go-viral), [Marketing Right Side Up](https://marketingrightsideup.beehiiv.com/p/dont-want-go-viral-mktrsu-012), [Reddit mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qqt793/snapshots/2026-01-31T04%3A50%3A42.947805Z)). Your own early viral post, the failed-attempts compilation, was reported as "inspiring others" ([Midpen Post](https://midpenpost.org/2024/05/17/flipping-in-finesse-gunn-juniors-mindset-grows-through-tricking/)), but no follower outcome was reported.

**Inference specific to tricking:**
1. **The audience came for the fall, not for you.** A crash is a self-contained payoff. After the fall, the viewer has no question left that your account answers.
2. **Spectators, not practitioners.** Most views on a crash are from non-trickers who will never follow a tricking account. Practitioners follow; spectators swipe.
3. **No promise on the profile.** A viewer who taps through to your profile sees whatever you posted last. If it is a random mix, "what do I get if I follow?" has no answer.
4. **The comeback was a separate post.** The landing "years later" paid off the crash, but a viewer of one never sees the other unless they connect. An arc only converts if both halves sit in one visible series.
5. **Followers need a repeat reason.** 奇軒 gives one: each Short is a new episode of the same story shape. Storror gives one: Monday 5pm, same faces ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2019/01/16/creators-going-pro-storror/)).

**Comparable cases:**
- Converted: 奇軒 (serial stories, under 10K → 1M in four months from Shorts, [CNA](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202403090019.aspx)); Storror (weekly slot, storytelling, merch, though it took ~7 years to reach 1M).
- Did not convert / unclear: Jason Paul's 158M views accrued on Red Bull's channel ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/jason-paul-best-freerunning-moments)). I found **no documented case** of a crash-viral tricking or parkour athlete who did/didn't convert with numbers. I am not going to invent one.

**Checks you can do in 10 minutes (analytics, not guessing):**
- On each platform: share of views from non-followers; profile visits per 1,000 views; follows per 1,000 views for the crash vs your average video.
- Which country the views came from (Japan vs US vs other).
- Whether the crash views were mostly "FYP/recommended" vs "following."
- Whether profile bio/pinned posts say what you post.

**Fix list (all zero-edit):**
1. Pin the crash and the landing as a visible pair; add a bio line like "Failed it in Kobe. Landed it years later. Learning triple cork next."
2. Turn "the road to X" into a numbered recurring series (section 5).
3. End videos with an open question you answer in the next one ("next: why I keep landing on my left foot").
4. Reply to the best comments from the crash video with new videos.

## 4. Format rotation (high volume, minimal editing)

Assumption: you film 1 to 2 training sessions per week and post about 10 to 14 pieces a week, same file cross-posted to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. No b-roll, no motion graphics; one trim and on-screen text (apps like CapCut auto-caption).

| # | Format | Tier | Edit load | Per week | What it builds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Attempt log**: raw clip of an attempt (fall or land) with one on-screen line "Triple cork, attempt 41" | General (series) | Trim only | 3-4 | Followable arc, episode count |
| B | **Fix-it**: you talk to camera in the gym, 20-40s, "why your X looks ugly", demo once | Specialist | One take | 3 | Authority, coaching leads |
| C | **Curiosity one-take**: sitting in the gym or dorm, one surprising claim and a 30s explanation | Informational | One take | 2-3 | Reach plus identity (Berkeley/CS) |
| D | **Student clip**: your student's before/after (with their permission), you comment | Specialist | Trim | 1-2 | Proof of coaching |
| E | **Rate/react**: stitch or duet a tricking clip, say what is good/bad | Specialist | Platform-native | 1-2 | Community |
| F | **Japan bilingual**: same clip with Japanese caption, or short Japanese explanation | Any | Caption only | 1-2 | Japan experiment |

Why high volume: the sources agree that conversion comes from many mid-performing posts more than one hit ([Reddit mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qqt793/snapshots/2026-01-31T04%3A50%3A42.947805Z)), and 奇軒 has 815 videos behind 15.8M subs ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCvw1LiGdyulhnGksJlGWB6g)).

Monetization route [inference, modelled on Heria Pro and 奇軒's classes]: free fix-it videos → "send me your clip" form check (paid) or a progression program → later an app/course. Do not add the sales step until follows per 1,000 views is above your current baseline.
