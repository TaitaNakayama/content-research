# 12. ごはんとも: what real Japanese food and diet Reels put on screen

For Taita (ごはんとも, mascot もぐもぐ). Local frame analysis only; no scraping, no web lookups.

Every earlier visual recommendation for ごはんとも was inference, because `11_instagram_visual_hooks.md` had no Japanese content. This file replaces that inference with observation from 20 Japanese Reels. Everything below was read off frames I opened. For each Reel I opened all seven hook-window frames (t0, t0_5, t1, t1_5, t2, t2_5, t3) plus up to six later frames spread across the Reel (sampled frames, not the whole video). Japanese is transcribed as printed. Marks: `†` means partly blurred or small, so my reading is less certain. `[判読不能]` means unreadable. Glosses and register labels are mine.

**Language check.** I selected every `jp_food` and `jp_diet` row (20). All 20 captions contain kana and none contains hangul. All on-screen text I opened was Japanese (two Reels mix in English labels). **No Reel was excluded as Korean.**

**Conventions**
- "Hook window" = t0 to t3. "Telop" = burned-in on-screen text.
- "Body number" = kg, cm, "キロ" or a before/after weight. "Other numeral" = time, age, date, count. kcal, grams and PFC are tracked separately.
- Likes are the manifest value (rounded). Five Reels have a blank likes field (DOVi, DOvU, C86k, DWP0, DPGT), so engagement comparisons use **15 Reels**.
- Vertical position is judged by eye from downscaled contact sheets (0% = top).
- One Reel (DHsN, an idol recording-studio vlog) is not food or diet content. It entered the diet bucket through the pun hashtag `#レコーディングダイエット`. I keep it in the table and in the counts, and flag it wherever it matters.

## 2. The telop playbook (first second)

**Presence.** 16 of 20 Reels show telop at t0. The other 4 (DfEy, DWP0, Dey38, DV8G) start it between 0.5 s and 1 s (DfEy types it in from a cursor). **20 of 20 have telop by 1 s.** I saw no Reel with a clean, text-free hook window.

**Length.** Per line, the t0 strings run 5 to 16 characters (examples: `痩せたいなら` 6, `これ作ったら` 6, `10分で作れる` 7, `天才的にうまい` 7, `食べても太られへんどころか` 13, `わが家はお風呂に入るだけで…` 14, `飲めるほど美味いアボカド豚しゃぶ` 16). 1 to 2 lines is the norm; DZXL (3 lines) and DMfX (a stats stack) are the exceptions. A typical hook telop is a fragment, not a sentence.

**Position.** By eye: about 12 of 20 sit in the vertical middle (roughly 40 to 60%, usually over the food or the person's torso). 3 sit high (DHQD ~30%, DSR9 ~27%, DMfX upper right). 2 sit low (DJ6V ~68%, DV8G ~70%). No Reel puts it at the very top or bottom safe-zone edge, apart from the DOVi and DMfX stat blocks near the top.

**Two-beat structure.** 7 of the 16 t0 telops are an open clause that the next telop completes. With DfEy and DV8G (which start slightly after t0) that is 9 of 20:
- `これ知らないの` (18K) → `もったいない！` at t2.5
- `これ作ったら` (38K) → `美味しすぎて` at t3
- `これ知らないダイエッター―` (512) → `損してます` at t1.5
- `食べても太られへんどころか` → `腸もスッキリ！` at t2.5
- `痩せたいなら` → `米の量を減らさないで` at t1
- `わが家はお風呂に入るだけで…` → `嫌でも体重が記録されます😲` at t3
- `ダイエット人生で` / `何回食べたかわからない。` (25K), typed out over t0.5 to t2
- `このキャベツの食べ方、` → `天才‥！！` at t1.5

**The telop changes inside the hook window in 16 of 20 Reels** (a new line every 0.5 to 2.5 s). It stays constant to t3 in only 4: DVX- (`153cm 71kg`), DGLF, DMfX and DJ6V. Those four are all "identity or stat label" telops rather than hooks.

**Register.** At t0, **0 of 16** telops use ですます. Every one is plain form, a noun phrase, a bare fragment or casual speech. Examples: `10分で作れる`, `天才的にうまい`, `お弁当が美味すぎる`, `食べても太られへんどころか`. ですます shows up in the hook window only twice (DOvU t2 `先日こんな質問が来てました`, C86k t3 `嫌でも体重が記録されます`) and later in step captions (`混ぜます`, `できあがりです`, `昼にはもう汗だくです`). The pattern: **plain voice for the hook, polite voice for instructions.** One Reel (DPGT) uses Kansai dialect throughout (`太られへん`, `忘れんうちに`); I did not see a Reel with a dialect on a non-Kansai account.

**Emoji in the hook window:** 4 of 20 (DVX- `💖`, DX6M `⏰` and `👨「…」`, DOVi plate and salad stickers, DGLF `✨`). Emoji are more common later: dish emoji, `‼`, `🥲`, `😍`, `🥰`, `💘`. Emoji at the end of a hook line is not the Japanese norm in this sample.

**Colour and style (visible).** White text dominates (all but one). Two looks recur:
- Thin white serif or light sans, about 40 to 50% down, with no outline. Seen in DSKR, DP5, DfEy, DebqKl, DV8G, DSR9, DZXL, DGLF.
- Bold white with a heavy dark outline, or a rounded "pop" font. Seen in DHQD (handwritten-pop, upper third), DJ6V, Dey38, DOvU, DVX- and DOVi (decorative or hand-drawn). DHsN uses white with a pink glow, the only coloured treatment at t0. DOvU highlights `200g` in yellow at t4.

**Quotes with like counts (verbatim, hook window).**
- 105K: `27歳の晩ごはん` / `飲めるほど美味いアボカド豚しゃぶ` / `を作った日`. At t0.5 it collapses to a clock `19:35` + `飲めるほど美味いアボカド豚しゃぶ`.
- 38K: `これ作ったら` → `美味しすぎて`
- 25K: `ダイエット人生で何回食べたかわからない。`
- 20K: `10分で作れる` → `ワンパン` → `絶品！ビビンバ丼`
- 20K: `20歳5時起き現場職人のお弁当が美味すぎる`
- 18K: `これ知らないの` → `もったいない！`
- 14K: `仕事帰りの夫がひとこと ⏰21:00` → `👨「この組み合わせ最高！」`
- 8,234: `天才的にうまい` → `旦那が大絶賛の` → `自由な青椒肉絲`
- 1,554: `家族みんなが絶賛` → `「これ炊飯器で？」` → `簡単で褒められる`

**Telop vs caption.** Telop and caption diverge in most Reels. The caption carries the dish name, ingredients and hashtags; the telop carries a reaction, a story frame or a claim. Telop mirrors the caption in only 3 of 20: DebqKl (spouse praise, "自由な青椒肉絲"), DSR9 (`10キロ痩せた` / `痩せチーズバーグ` both in caption), DOvU (the caption's "お米を減らすと痩せない理由"). In DP5 the caption says `\太らない/ 生チョコケーキ` but the on-screen hook says only `これ知らないの` / `もったいない！`; the telop is softer than the caption.

**Payoff placement.** All 13 food Reels (11 recipes + 2 meal logs) show the finished dish, or someone eating it, **inside the hook window** (e.g., DZXL eats at t1.5 to t3, DP5 cuts the cake at t0 to t3, DSR9 opens on the finished tray). Steps follow, and the Reel typically closes by returning to the plated dish or a profile/follow card at 0.8 to 1.0 of the duration (DZXL's end card at t60 of 61.3). Step captions then carry quantities: `醤油大さじ2` (DZXL), `鶏がら 小さじ1` (DfEy), `ピーマン5個 細切りに` (DHQD), `米2合` / `牛肉250g` / `バター10g` (Dey38). Quantities are in later telop for 4 of 11 recipes (sampled frames only).

**Difference from the English Reels in `11_instagram_visual_hooks.md`.**
- English: headline words at t0 in 20 of 52 (38%), no overlay in the hook window in 21 of 52 (40%). In that file text rate was set by format: 23% of visual-demo Reels vs 100% of speech/text-led. Its 3 food-process Reels had overlay in only 1.
- Japanese: telop by 1 s in 20 of 20, and in all 13 food-process Reels. A "no overlay" food Reel is the exception in English and absent here (0 of 13). I caution that the English food-process n is 3.
- English hook text was short (8 of 14 were four words or fewer) and often ended with an emoji (7 of 14). Japanese telop is a fragment of 6 to 16 characters, emoji are rare at t0, and two-beat setup-then-punch is common.
- English hook text is mostly a label for the clip. Japanese hook text is mostly a reaction, a story frame, a time or a claim about the cook (`旦那が大絶賛の`, `仕事帰りの夫がひとこと`, `20歳5時起き現場職人の`).
- `11` said Japanese conventions were "untested". They are now observed: on-screen text from the first second is the convention for this genre.

## 5. What もぐもぐ should do (from observation only)

1. **Put text on screen from t0.** 20 of 20 had telop by 1 s. A text-free hook would be against the grain of this genre, not neutral.
2. **Write it as a fragment, 6 to 14 characters, in plain form.** 0 of 16 t0 telops used ですます. A second telop resolves it at about 1 to 3 s (9 of 20 follow this setup-then-punch shape). Switch to ですます for steps or explanation.
3. **Show the result first.** 13 of 13 food Reels show the finished dish or the eating in the first 3 s. Steps follow. For a calorie app the analogue is the verdict screen, not the camera-shutter step.
4. **Use the number slot, carefully.** Creators fill the first-second slot with a number in 10 of 20 Reels, but with body numbers (5), time or age (5), and kcal in none. This is the slot where the app's own verdict could sit: for example a photo and a short result line such as `約〇〇kcal` or a verdict chip. That would be a genuinely different number from what these creators show. No Reel I looked at did it, so there is no evidence that it works; it is an untested gap. If tested, compare it against a time number (`10分で…`), which is the numeral most visible in the high-like recipe Reels.
5. **Timestamps are a native device.** `19:35`, `21:00`, `10:00 / 15:00 / 17:30` appear in 3 Reels (DZXL, DX6M, DOVi) as meal-time stamps. A meal log with timestamps would look familiar to this audience.
6. **Screen-record the app, but not at t0.** In C86k the phone UI only appears at about 0.13 to 0.66 of the duration, after a text hook and a product shot. The app demo is the middle, not the opener.
7. **Do not lead with a weight number.** The Reels that do (DOVi, DVX-, DGLF, DMfX, DSR9) are all small accounts here, and they raise the compliance issues in section 6. The product also keeps weight values out of analytics by design, which is a reason to keep them off the hook.
8. **Mascot.** No mascot appears in any of the 20, so a character-led hook is a differentiation, not a proven pattern. In-app strings still come only from the screen-list §5 copy keys; the telop wording in this file is for creative or ad testing, not for in-app text.

## 6. Compliance flags (what I actually saw; not legal advice)

| Seen on screen (Reel, likes) | Why it is risky for an app | Safer Japanese direction |
|---|---|---|
| `10キロ痩せた` + `やせチーズバーグ` over a waist-pinch with oversized jeans (DSR9, 512) | Concrete weight-loss result tied to a food, plus a before/after image. Reads as an efficacy claim (景品表示法 優良誤認, 健康増進法 誇大表示; 薬機法 if a product were implied). | `毎日の食事を記録して、食べ方を見直しました` / `体験談です。効果には個人差があります` |
| `70kg→45kg` + `＼半年で−20kg！／` with face before/after thumbnails (DOVi, no likes value) | Numerical result promise plus before/after. The two numbers in the frame disagree (25 kg vs 20 kg). Personal-result claims need a basis and a 個人差 note (景表法). | `半年間、毎日の食事を記録しました（個人の感想です）` |
| `食べても太られへんどころか` + `腸もスッキリ！` (DPGT, no likes value) | "Eat and don't gain weight" is an absolute claim, and `腸もスッキリ` is a body-effect claim for a food (薬機法 / 健康増進法 territory). | `食物繊維が入ったレシピです` / `罪悪感少なめに楽しめます` |
| `-8kg ✨ 一日の食事` (DGLF, 389) | Personal result next to a meal plan, no 個人差 note seen in the sampled frames. | `記録をつけて、一日の食事を振り返りました` |
| `1日目 54.1kg … 36日目 51.7kg 骨格ウェーブ` (DMfX, 73) | Day 1 vs day 36 weight shown as numbers; a mild personal record, but still a before/after. | Fine as a personal log; for an app say `36日間の記録` without a kg figure. |
| `痩せたいなら` / `米の量を減らさないで` / `健康的に痩せるための` (DOvU) | Advice framed as a path to weight loss. Acceptable as opinion; risky if the app echoes it as a promise. (The caption also has `整形レベルで外見が変わるボディメイク術`, which I read in the manifest, not on a frame.) | `食事の記録でカロリーの目安がわかります` |
| `\太らない/ 生チョコケーキ` (DP5, 18K) | This is **in the caption, not a frame I saw.** "Doesn't make you fat" is an absolute claim. The on-screen hook itself (`これ知らないの` / `もったいない！`) is milder. | `ダイエット中でも楽しめる、ヘルシーなおやつ` |
| `これ知らないダイエッター―` / `損してます` (DSR9) | Loss-aversion and shaming framing aimed at dieters. Not illegal in itself, but poor fit for a supportive mascot. | `知ってた？ 〇〇の食べ方` |
| `購入品紹介〜💖` over a Rakuten logo (DVX-); Rakuten-room links in C86k and DWP0 captions | Affiliate or ad content. Under the 2023 ステマ規制 (景表法), sponsored posts need a clear disclosure. I saw no `PR` or `広告` label in the sampled frames, but I cannot tell whether these were sponsored. | If an app ad is sponsored, label it clearly (`PR` / `広告`). |

**For the app:** keep any weight or kg numbers out of public-facing hooks, avoid `痩せる` / `太らない` as outcomes, and phrase verdicts as an estimate of what was eaten (`目安`), not as an effect on the body. Check anything customer-facing with a Japanese-law specialist.

## 7. Honest limits

- **Sample size.** 20 Reels (19 on topic). Sub-groups are small, so treat all of these as provisional (under about 6): body-number Reels (4 with likes), diet-record Reels (2 to 4), meal logs (2), talking-head (1), product (3), person-first with likes (5).
- **Engagement skew.** One Reel at 105K, six more at 14K to 38K, then a long tail: of the 15 with likes, 7 are under 2K (down to 73). Medians in sections 3 and 4 swing on one or two Reels.
- **Five Reels have no likes value** (DOVi, DOvU, C86k, DWP0, DPGT), so they are in the counts of what is on screen but not in any engagement comparison.
- **Uneven food vs diet split.** The 10/10 bucket split is misleading: the diet bucket includes an idol vlog, a café-news Reel, a clothes haul and a bath-mat ad. True diet-record content is a handful of small accounts. The "recipe" group is dominated by large recipe creators, so format and account size are tangled.
- **No audio.** Many of these Reels likely have voiceover or music that shapes the hook; I saw none of it. Talking-head DOvU and DPGT's person eating on camera depend on speech I could not hear.
- **Sampled frames only.** The hook window is read at 0.5 s steps; after t3 only up to six frames per Reel were opened. Short telops between samples, and the full run of step captions, may be missed. Positions and colours are by eye from downscaled contact sheets, and a few small strings are marked `†`.
- **Likes are not reach.** Likes are rounded, the manifest and caption sometimes disagree by a small amount (DfEy 25K vs 26K; DebqKl 8,234 vs 8,237), and none of this measures views, saves or follows. I did not check any of it against watch time.
- **Correlation only.** Nothing here shows that a telop pattern causes engagement. It shows what these 20 Reels do.
