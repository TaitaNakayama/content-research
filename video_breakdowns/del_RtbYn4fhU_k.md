## Transferable lesson for taita.del
The only Short in this corpus with a clearly worded contrarian hook, and it works by stating the reader's lack ("If you can't...") rather than the creator's skill. Taita could invert this for beginners with a spoken "if you can't land a [trick], here's the one drill my students do first", using the coaching base as the reason to trust it. The repeated phrase gives the Short a spoken wrapper with no credentials at all, which is also the weakness: nothing here converts a viewer into a follower, because there is no CTA and no identity. One Short, 1.1M views; not enough to generalise.
