## 2. Japanese hook formulas that recur in speech
- **Quoted viewer question, then answer** (all three Shorts by 2 channels): 「痩せたいので夜ご飯抜いた方がいいですか」(HEWj4qFElYQ), 「GLP-1ダイエットって痩せますよね」(RzdydY-2mbM), 「カロリーメイトは健康に悪いって本当」(HkrjA21vcFw). Claim starts 0.04-2.4s. The expected answer is flipped almost at once (「そういうことするからいつまで当たっても痩せないのさ」, 「痩せないし100%リバウンドする」).
- **Myth stated flat**: 「コンビニサラダやカット野菜は栄養がない」(75Kl0f6Iv5w), claim at 0.12s.
- **Greeting-then-premise** (every long-form): 「こんにちはシェイどうもやまももですダイエット中の食事」(caVFRKCk-ec), 「どうも君まろです…セブンイレブンの美味しい高タンパクなダイエット食を紹介していこう」(2dMk5UK1R-k), 「ダイエッターの皆さんこんにちはこんばんは」(OK9NMaZcP_E). First claim at 6-26s.
- **Verdict-first** (one case): 「ジェシカさん太りすぎですBMI…第2肥満です」(L2_EVHN-ppY, the highest-view video, 645,456). A score/verdict before the story.
- **Challenge declaration** after a deferred weight promise: 「後で体重公開するんやけど」…「今回は1週間コンビニのダイエット向き商品を食べて生活してみようと思います」(6BbAzYQQFOs, 0:15 and 0:19).
- **Community close** 「一緒に」: 「今日も一緒に」(6BbAzYQQFOs), 「カロリー管理一緒に頑張りましょう」(KjeK4gA8mFw).
- Long-form closes are CTA stacks: 「チャンネル登録」 + SNS follow, and 「lineの友だち追加」 (6BbAzYQQFOs), 「Instagramにも遊びに来てください」(OK9NMaZcP_E).

## 3. Do the research claims hold up?
**Claimed formula `身長+体重 + 1日のご飯 + emoji`**: **cannot be tested from speech.** None of the 15 spoken openings recites 身長+体重; that formula is a title/on-screen-text device. The corpus contains no 163cm42kg-style Shorts (the nominated food-log items, e.g. ZFJ16jmfGkQ, have a title that does not use it, and its transcript is nearly empty: 46 words in 9:43). Verdict: **unsupported and uncontradicted** by this evidence. What the transcripts do show is that weight-in-the-voice appears in the weigh-in format (6BbAzYQQFOs, F7B3sTHs6lk: 「1日目53.4キロ」「4日目54キロ 前日より+0.4キロ増」), a per-day weight/meal/exercise log, which is the spoken analogue.

**"Permission-giving beats discipline"**: **partly supported, partly contradicted, mostly untestable.**
- Supports: 「食べて痩せる」(6BbAzYQQFOs, 14:13); the prescriptions 「夜ご飯はしっかり食べてください」 and 「和食定食を意識すれば普通に痩せるから」(HEWj4qFElYQ, RzdydY-2mbM); 「別に痩せたくないからね健康のためやから」(L2_EVHN-ppY, ~26:13); 「食べ過ぎた次の日だろうと…」 tone in F7B3sTHs6lk.
- Contradicts/qualifies: the Short *hooks* are warnings and rebukes, not permission (「夕食抜きがあなたを太らせる」, 「絶対やめ…」), and the highest-view video opens with an insult-style BMI verdict. So in speech, **contrarian/verdict openings win; permission appears in the answer, not the hook.**
- Untestable: the research's evidence was titles (「無理せず」「ゆるダイエット」); ZFJ16jmfGkQ's 「我慢しすぎず」 title is not echoed in its (sparse) speech.

**Correlation to views is not shown.** The top-2 are 体重公開 and diet-record long-forms; the Shorts that open with a question did 18k-95k. Sample is too small and views are channel-driven.

## 4. Register norms (what creators actually sound like)
- Long-form vlog creators: **casual-polite**, ですます at greeting/close with plain/dialect asides (Kansai: 「やんか」「ほんま」「ええんよな」「覚えきられへん」). L2, caVFRKCk, 6Bb.
- Male fitness creators: **ですます throughout** (2dMk5UK1R-k, TSYCATVYAPk, OK9NMaZcP_E).
- Question-and-rebuke Shorts: **casual plain, blunt** (「〜のさ」「〜から絶対やめ」), with ですます only in the quoted question.
- Media/dietitian Shorts (MELOS): **neutral written narration**, plain-form 「〜しよう」 plus occasional 「おすすめ」.
- Gentle "stress-free" vlog (ZFJ16jmfGkQ): **very casual friend-talk** (「見て」「じゃん」).
- Implication: nobody sounds like a clinical coach. A ですます brand voice with friendly plain asides fits.

## 6. What もぐもぐ should and should not say
**Should:** 
- Open with a verdict/question about a *meal or number*, not a body: 「このコンビニ飯、何kcal？」 / the app's score for the meal. L2 shows verdict-first works; pointing it at food rather than a person removes the risk.
- Use ですます with friendly plain asides; speak "一緒に" (「一緒に記録しよう」).
- Claim within 3s (the Shorts did it in 0.04-2.4s).
- Disclose error: 「AIの推定値、誤差があります」 (compare Smw49EoRPW0's over/underestimate hedge).
- Spoken log structure like F7B3sTHs6lk (date, meals, note), and 「食べたものを毎日記録するのは大事」 as the benefit.
- Add a LINE/Instagram prompt in long-form closes only; Shorts here have none.

**Should not:** say 痩せる/〇kg/〇ヶ月 in the app's own voice, mention GLP-1/medication, use 100%/絶対/必ず, claim beauty/metabolism effects, or judge bodies.

**Not answerable from this data:** on-screen text style, emoji density, thumbnails, slideshow performance, whether 身長+体重 titles work. A telop/title sample from actual frames or the platform UI is needed.
