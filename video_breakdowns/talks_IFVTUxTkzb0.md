## One-take feasibility
No. The structure depends on on-screen demos of an AI tool, a sponsor read and a played-back clip (all referenced in the speech). A talk-only version would be one step at a time, from memory.

## Transferable lesson for taita.talks
Gohar's authority is borrowed from famous student founders in the first 45 seconds and his own credential is held back until 10:30. That is the workaround for a 19-year-old: he can teach "what other founders did" and add what he sees building at Berkeley without claiming results. The "six steps" skeleton is one-take friendly if shortened; the demo-heavy middle is not.
