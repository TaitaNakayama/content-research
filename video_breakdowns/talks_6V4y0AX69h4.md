## Transferable lesson for taita.talks
The dedication line ("for everyone who's been told they can't") works without a business résumé, which suits a 19-year-old. The "famous story, then my own small version" loop is the useful part; it lets Taita use his tricking rank and student-founder experiences as the small version. What hurts: heavy clip borrowing and 15 minutes.
