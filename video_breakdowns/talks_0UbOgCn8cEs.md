## Transferable lesson for taita.talks
It is the shortest complete unit in the sample: quote, rule, reason. Taita doesn't run a company, so "in my company" doesn't fit, but "my one unbreakable rule in training" or "in my startup team" does, and the quote-first opening lends authority he doesn't need to earn. The risk is the pronouncement tone ("winners ...") reads as authority a 19-year-old hasn't established; frame the rule as his own practice, not a law.
