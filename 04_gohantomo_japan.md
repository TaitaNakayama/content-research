# Gohantomo (ごはんとも) — Japan content research

Written for Taita. Evidence rules apply: figures marked (unverified) came from search snippets or self-reported founder claims. `[inference]` = my own reasoning. **Not reachable from this environment:** TikTok and Instagram pages (blocked or 429), Markezine (403), X (402). So I could not read what asken or Calomeal actually post, and I could not verify any Japanese photo-slideshow view counts. Where evidence is thin I say so.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Whitespace is real.** The two big Japanese food-log apps have small owned social. Asken's IG is ~37K (unverified). Calomeal has ~7K IG and ~6K X, and I found no Calomeal TikTok. Neither seems to run a TikTok mascot-plus-slideshow engine. Their growth appears to come from product, TV and user posts. [inference]
2. **The US slideshow playbook is not proven in Japan.** I found no Japanese case with verifiable numbers. Japanese sources describe photo mode as a utility/catalog format. Treat it as a hypothesis to test in the first 30 posts, not a plan.
3. **Compliance is the biggest Japan-specific risk.** Avoid "痩せる" promises, kg-loss claims and before/after images, and keep PR/ステマ labelling clean (section 5).

## 2. Do US-style app slideshows work in Japan?

### What the US apps actually did (claims, mostly self-reported)
- **Cal AI** (founder statements via [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/)): creators at ~50K+ followers with tight communities. A first ~$2,000 story post gave ~1,000 downloads in two days. He called UGC "spray and pray" and influencers "sniper". Revenue of $30M/yr was claimed. Secondary blogs say Cal AI ran 12+ brand-owned accounts in Jan 2025 mixing carousels and faceless video (low reliability).
  - **Takeaway: Cal AI's core growth was paid creators, then paid ads. It was not a single brand account posting slideshows.** The slideshow part is the weakest-evidenced piece.
- **Rizz** (founder post via [youmind mirror](https://youmind.com/it-IT/landing/x-viral-articles/rizz-app-faceless-creator-growth); self-reported): faceless creators paid $1–2 per 1,000 views, with text-message-story videos. Claimed 5B views, 300K+ videos, $15M+ revenue. This is **video with text bubbles, not photo slideshows.** Also see the [Shortimize teardown](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/rizz-apps-b-roll-strategy-that-got-them-half-a-billion-views-on-tiktok).
- **Umax:** only a low-quality blog result. Nothing usable.

### Japanese evidence on photo mode
- Japanese practitioner blogs call it 「フォトモード」, 2–35 images, up to 4,000-character captions ([active-note](https://www.active-note.jp/sns/photomode/)). One blog says reach "doubled" in one producer's experience, anecdotal with no data.
- Real Japanese brand uses I could verify are **catalogs and announcements**: Gong cha swipeable menu guide ([@gongcha_japan](https://www.tiktok.com/@gongcha_japan)), Lilyanna lens colour comparisons ([@lilyanna.jp](https://www.tiktok.com/@lilyanna.jp)) per [Comnico](https://www.comnico.jp/we-love-social/best-tiktok-posted-case-2025); also GU, Uniqlo, Sushiro, Toyota per [utakata](https://utakata.co.jp/media/tiktok-photo/). No view numbers anywhere.
- Of Comnico's 18 best 2025 cases, 16 are video and only 2 are photo mode. [inference] Video still dominates Japanese brand wins.
- Japan has a strong 保存 (save) culture on Instagram carousels. [inference, not sourced here]

### Verdict
**Unproven, plausible, cheap to test.** The format travels as a utility/reference format (menus, comparisons, "save this" lists), which fits calorie content well. It is unproven as a growth engine. What changes in Japan:
1. **Hooks must be utility, not shock.** Swap US-style drama for 「保存版」「〇〇kcal早見表」.
2. **Never promise a body result** (section 5).
3. **Add a face (もぐもぐ)** because faceless-account trust is weaker for health claims. [inference]
4. **Run a hedge:** make 1 in 3 posts a short video (もぐもぐ animation or your voice) so you aren't betting on one format. Also post the best slideshows as Instagram carousels.

Risks: AI-generated content must be labelled and PR transparency is stricter since TikTok's Aug 2025 guideline update (per a search summary of [stock-sun](https://stock-sun.com/?p=45405), unverified); a Japanese health-claim crackdown; diet content skewing toward minors (see below).

## 3. Mascot-led accounts in Japan

Evidence base is [Comnico's character-account guide](https://www.comnico.jp/we-love-social/character-account) plus [Oricon](https://www.oricon.co.jp/special/54774/) and [hashout](https://hashout.jp/?p=12312). I could not read mascot TikToks.

| Account | What it does | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| 日清 ひよこちゃん (X) | Surreal, self-deprecating, "venting" persona; one post >100K likes (Oricon). Persona built from accumulated posts, not one hit (hashout). | Give もぐもぐ a *flaw* (always hungry, bad at saying no to ramen). |
| SMBC ミドすけ | Ties to calendar days (e.g. 喫茶店の日), relatable daily-life takes. | Use food-day hooks (〇〇の日). |
| Zespri Kiwi Brothers | High frequency, entertainment with no overt promotion. | Most posts should not mention the app. |
| くまモン | Real-life "staff" photos. | A mascot can act as an employee. |
| Expo 2025 ミャクミャク | Observed from a third-person view rather than speaking as the character. | Alternate option if a first-person voice feels forced. |

**Comnico checklist for もぐもぐ:** first-person pronoun (ボク/わたし), politeness level, emoji rules, post energy, trend rules, banned topics, reply policy. Sample ratio 8:2 everyday vs promotional.
**Risks (same source):** staff changes make the character inconsistent, and a controversy hits the brand. You are the single operator, so write the persona sheet once.
**My take [inference]:** もぐもぐ is "the food that eats with you". Make him narrate the photo ("これ、500kcalくらい？" then reveal), never a body-shaming scold. Fits an app named ごはんとも ("meal buddy").

This is also the safer legal and ethical frame.

## 5. Conventions and compliance

**Compliance (flag for a real shipping app; I am not a lawyer, get 薬機法/景表法 review before launch):**
- **薬機法 (Pharmaceutical and Medical Device Act):** applies to products with claimed medical effects. Before/after images and guarantees are restricted for cosmetics and drugs ([Monolith](https://monolith.law/corporate/beauty-diet-advertising-restrictions)). Whether it applies to a calorie app depends on whether you claim medical/therapeutic effect. Don't claim treatment, 予防 or 治療, and don't claim "diagnoses" or "medical-grade" accuracy.
- **景品表示法 (Premiums and Representations Act):** 優良誤認 (misleading quality claims) is banned. Penalties include 措置命令 and surcharges of up to 3% of sales (per [atsoho](https://atsoho.com/blog/trainer-before-after-ad-rules) summary; verify). Diet items already see enforcement.
- **Practical rules [inference]:** no "〇kg痩せる", no "確実", no time-bound promises ("1ヶ月で"), no user results as the lead claim (Calomeal's "-5.3kg" is the pattern to avoid unless you have substantiation), no before/after photos, and state accuracy claims ("誤差±〇%") only with test evidence.
- **ステマ (undisclosed ads) rules:** in force since Oct 2023, from my background knowledge (not verified here). Label creator and PR posts "PR".
- **AI accuracy:** say 「目安」 (estimate), since AI photo kcal can be wrong.
- **Minors/eating-disorder risk:** no extreme-restriction content, no ideal-weight numbers.

**Culture and style [inference, not sourced; thin evidence]:**
- Tone: ですます softened with casual endings for the mascot; avoid である.
- Katakana for food words and emphasis; kaomoji and emoji fit the mascot (decide the rules in the persona sheet).
- On-screen text: short, one idea per slide. The English "5–6 words per slide" advice doesn't map to Japanese. Aim for ~10–15 characters a line, [inference].
- LINE is the retention/CRM channel in Japan, so consider a LINE official account for 「毎日記録」 reminders. Not researched.
